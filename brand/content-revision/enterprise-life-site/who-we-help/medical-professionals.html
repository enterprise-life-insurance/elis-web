Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my specialty matter for disability coverage?

Generic occupation categories can undervalue specialized training. Coverage should reflect what it would actually cost to replace your specific skill set and income, not a broad average.

I still have student debt — how does that factor in?

Outstanding debt doesn't disappear if you're unable to work. Coverage can be structured to specifically cover those payments so they don't become a burden on your family.

I own my practice — what happens to overhead if I can't work?

Overhead expense insurance covers fixed costs like rent, staff wages, and equipment leases while you're unable to work, so the practice can stay open until you're back.

Can I set up group benefits for my staff?

Yes — group benefits plans can be structured for practices of any size, including clinics with just a few employees.
