# ELIS — Brand Voice & Messaging (Final, locked 2026-08-03)

## What we stand for

- Honest and transparent advice
- Solutions tailored to your unique needs
- Ongoing guidance as life changes
- Trusted long-term relationships
- Comprehensive insurance and financial solutions under one roof

## Positioning statement

> Enterprise Life Insurance and Investment Solutions provides personalized financial and estate planning, insurance, and group benefits solutions for business owners, professionals, and families. Our advisors bring decades of hands-on experience, helping clients translate complex risks into clear, actionable plans.

## Mission statement

> Our mission is to help clients protect their loved ones, support their employees, and achieve lasting financial security with confidence and clarity.

## Vision statement

> Our vision is to be the trusted advisor for individuals, families, and business owners by empowering them with knowledge, personalized planning, and strategies that protect what matters most.

## Communication pillars

**1. Clarity Over Complexity** *(Honest & Transparent Advice)*
- Say: "Here's exactly what this policy covers."
- Not: "We believe in transparency."

**2. Built Around You** *(Tailored Solutions)*
- Say: "A business owner and a tradesperson need different coverage — here's how."
- Not: "Comprehensive solutions for everyone."

**3. In It for the Long Run** *(Ongoing Guidance)*
- Say: "Your plan changes when your life does."
- Not: "Get your free quote today."

**4. Earned Trust, Not Claimed Trust** *(Trusted Long-Term Relationships)*
- Say: "Jay's been writing policies since 1994."
- Not: "We're the most trusted advisor in the industry."

**5. One Advisor, Every Angle** *(Comprehensive Coverage)*
- Say: "One advisor handled her life insurance, benefits, and estate plan together."
- Not: "We offer life, health, travel insurance, and more."

## Segment tone

- **Engineers** — Authority/Ruler inflection. Analytical, precise, cut-to-the-point. Lead with numbers and logic.
- **Healthcare** — Caregiver inflection. Empathetic but efficient.
- **Business owners & contractors** — Sage + practical. Speak to risk to the business itself.
- **Families & Individuals** — Caregiver + Sage. Warmest of the four; centers protection and peace of mind.

## Core brand tension

New entity, not new advisors. Jay Bablani (since 1994) and Glenn Merkley (30+ years) bring decades of experience into a freshly formed company — the newness of ELIS must never read as inexperience. This has to come through on the website.
